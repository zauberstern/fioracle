01 What It Does, In One Sentence

Most fixed income tools chase a return that the academic literature says barely exists. FIOracle stopped chasing it, and built one honestly measurable thing instead: a portfolio that is designed to fall less than the market when the market breaks.

How most tools compete

Stack ten cutting-edge methods -- foundation models, graph neural nets, reinforcement learning -- and market the combination as an edge. It reads well in a pitch deck. It rarely survives contact with live capital.

Not our approach
VS

How FIOracle competes

One metric, defined before the fact: crisis-conditional drawdown behavior. Every method we add -- regime detection, calibrated uncertainty, a volatility overlay -- is tested against that one metric, or it does not ship.

Our approach

This is a positioning choice, not a modesty performance. Residual alpha in active fixed income management, once you strip out factor premia, is close to zero across most manager categories in the published literature. We built around that fact instead of ignoring it.

We do not publish a public Sharpe or ERoD number on this site. When the gate clears, figures would still be hypothetical performance with audience and disclosure rules. See Compliance.

04 A Second Return Stream, Gated on Purpose

An optional volatility-premium overlay, deliberately leashed, because free lunches in this corner of the market have a body count.

Term structure: contango
Crisis probability: low
HARVEST: ON

Fifteen percent of every dollar harvested is diverted into a tail-hedge asset, untouched by the harvest itself, in case the field burns. This sleeve is off by default (vrp_overlay.enabled: false, live_enabled: false) and is ETF total-return only -- a listed short-vol ETF total-return series, not a buy recommendation.
